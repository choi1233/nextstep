Motion design...
extend visual communication by adding 'time' and 'movement' to a static canvas. Unlike flat designs, which rely on spatial layouts for hierarchical structures, videos use timelines to design temporal hierarchies that guide viewers' cognitive flows. Here, movement goes beyond mere effects and acts as a visual language to express emotions. In short, video design is a form of advanced information design that combines the core principles of visual design with the directing power of time.
